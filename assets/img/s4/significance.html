You're comparing 6 users against 4 users. That is far too few to tell a real difference from plain luck. Think of flipping a coin 6 times and getting 4 heads. That doesn't prove the coin is loaded.

An 8-point gap on ~10 people could easily flip if you added just two or three more users to either side. To trust this, you'd want roughly hundreds per variant, not single digits.
