3 weaknesses
  1. Vague claims. "Faster" and "much easier" carry no numbers. Add the metric (e.g. 3x faster load) or cut them.
  2. Unverified superlative. "Huge step forward" is opinion, not fact. A skeptical reviewer will flag it.
  3. No audience or next step. It never says who this is for or what to do (try it / read the docs).
