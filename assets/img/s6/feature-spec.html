Support reply drafter

1Job to be done
Draft a first-pass support reply from a customer ticket, for an agent to review.
2Inputs + source
The ticket text + the linked order record. Read-only.
3Output shape
One reply, 3-5 sentences, plain text. No refund promises.
4"Good" = 3 criteria
Grounded in ticket + order . correct next step . calm on-brand tone.
5Failure modes
Invents an order status . promises a refund . rude or robotic tone.
